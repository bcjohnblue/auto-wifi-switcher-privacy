# Auto Wi-Fi Switcher 隱私政策 / Privacy Policy

最後更新 / Last updated: 2026-10-26

## 繁體中文

Auto Wi-Fi Switcher（以下稱「本 App」）由個人開發者 bcjohn 開發。本 App 在您的裝置上運作，**沒有後端伺服器**。除非您主動送出「問題回報」（見下方），本 App 不會上傳任何資料。

### 我們存取的資料與用途

| 資料 | 用途 | 儲存位置 |
|---|---|---|
| 附近 Wi-Fi 的掃描結果（名稱、基地台位址、訊號強度） | 判斷是否有訊號更強的 Wi-Fi 可切換 | 僅在記憶體中處理，不保存 |
| 定位權限（含背景） | Android 規定讀取 Wi-Fi 掃描結果必須持有定位權限；背景定位讓監測在重開機後自動恢復。本 App **不會取得、計算或儲存您的地理位置** | 不適用 |
| 您在 App 中輸入的 Wi-Fi 密碼（僅自動模式） | 交給 Android 系統以自動連線 | 以 Android Keystore 的硬體金鑰 AES-GCM 加密後存於裝置本機，排除於雲端備份，絕不傳送 |
| 切換紀錄（時間、來源與目標 Wi-Fi、訊號） | 顯示最近一次切換與切換紀錄 | 裝置本機資料庫，30 天後自動刪除，也可在切換紀錄頁手動清除 |
| 購買狀態 | 判斷 Pro 權益 | 由 Google Play 提供，本 App 僅快取「是否為 Pro」 |

### 問題回報（僅在您按下「送出」時）

在「⋮ → 問題回報」按下「送出」時，本 App 才會將以下內容傳送到開發者的 Google 表單（儲存於 Google 試算表，僅開發者可檢視），用於排查問題與評估功能需求：

- 您填寫的內容：回報類型、描述、Email（選填）、Android 版本、手機型號
- 診斷資訊：App 版本與設定、權限狀態、監測執行狀況、最近 7 天切換結果（僅時間、結果與訊號強度）、App 被系統關閉的原因，以及裝置資訊（製造商與型號、Android 版本與安全性更新、背景限制與省電狀態、Wi-Fi 功能與目前連線的頻率與訊號、記憶體、螢幕、語言與時區、電量、安裝來源）

診斷資訊**不含** Wi-Fi 名稱、基地台位址、Wi-Fi 密碼、地理位置、廣告 ID 或任何裝置識別碼。按下「送出」前不會收集或傳送任何資料。若要刪除您送出的回報，請來信聯絡。

### 第三方服務

- **Google Play 結算服務**：處理 Pro 的購買，並在換機或重新安裝後自動恢復，受 Google 隱私政策規範。
- **Google 表單**（僅在您送出問題回報時）：接收回報內容，受 Google 隱私政策規範。
- **Google AdMob**（僅免費版）：顯示橫幅廣告，可能收集廣告 ID 與裝置資訊以投放廣告。在法規要求的地區，本 App 會先透過 Google 的 User Messaging Platform 徵詢您的同意，您可於「進階設定 → 隱私選項」隨時變更。購買 Pro 後不再載入任何廣告 SDK 請求。

### 資料刪除

移除本 App 或在系統設定中清除資料，即可刪除所有本機資料（含加密的 Wi-Fi 密碼與切換紀錄）。已送出的問題回報請來信要求刪除。

## English

Auto Wi-Fi Switcher ("the app") is developed by an individual developer, bcjohn. The app runs entirely on your device. **There is no backend server.** Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to send a problem report (see below).

### Data we access and why

| Data | Purpose | Where it is kept |
|---|---|---|
| Nearby Wi-Fi scan results (name, access point address, signal strength) | Decide whether a stronger Wi-Fi is available | Processed in memory only, not stored |
| Location permission (including background) | Android requires location permission to read Wi-Fi scan results; background location lets monitoring resume after a reboot. The app **never obtains, computes or stores your geographic position** | N/A |
| Wi-Fi passwords you enter in the app (automatic mode only) | Handed to Android so it can connect automatically | Encrypted on device with an AES-GCM key held in the Android Keystore, excluded from cloud backup, never transmitted |
| Switch log (time, source and target Wi-Fi, signal) | Show the latest switch and the switch log | Local database on device, deleted automatically after 30 days; you can also clear it on the switch log screen |
| Purchase state | Determine Pro entitlement | Provided by Google Play; the app only caches "is Pro" |

### Problem reports (only when you tap Send)

Only when you tap Send under ⋮ → Report a problem does the app send the following to the developer's Google Form (stored in a Google Sheet that only the developer can see), to investigate issues and weigh feature requests:

- What you fill in: report type, description, email (optional), Android version and phone model
- Diagnostics: app version and settings, permission status, monitoring status, switch results from the last 7 days (time, outcome and signal strength only), the reasons the system closed the app, and device information (manufacturer and model, Android version and security patch, background restriction and battery saver state, Wi-Fi capabilities and the current connection's frequency and signal, memory, screen, language and time zone, battery level, install source)

Diagnostics **never include** Wi-Fi names, access point addresses, Wi-Fi passwords, your location, the advertising ID or any device identifier. Nothing is collected or sent before you tap Send. To have a report you sent deleted, email us.

### Third-party services

- **Google Play Billing** handles Pro purchases and restores them automatically after a phone change or reinstall, under Google's privacy policy.
- **Google Forms** (only when you send a problem report) receives the report, under Google's privacy policy.
- **Google AdMob** (free version only) shows a banner ad and may collect the advertising ID and device information to serve ads. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent first through Google's User Messaging Platform; you can change your choice any time under Advanced settings → Privacy options. Once you buy Pro, no ad SDK requests are made.

### Deleting your data

Uninstall the app or clear its data in system settings to delete everything stored locally, including encrypted Wi-Fi passwords and the switch log. To delete a problem report you sent, email us.
